Everything else in `assets/` (Jelly and other enemy sheets, weapons, player sprites, money, and the older `Dungeon*.png` tiles, no longer used by the game) was drawn by Vincent Barr, per the team. `stone-ceiling.png` and `pit.png` were drawn pixel by pixel with a script, in the palette colors.

Derived from the team's art: the Mage player sheets (`assets/player/mage-*.png`, recolored and redrawn from the 16x16 and 16x32 player sheets) and the enemy sheets Jelly, Bouncer, Spark Bark, Digger and Bat, which were recolored in place to the game palette.

Drawn for this project: `assets/item/weapon-icons.png`, `assets/item/item-icons.png`, `assets/item/rarity-frames.png` and `assets/effects/effects.png`.
